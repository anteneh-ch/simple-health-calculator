BEDSIDE REFERENCE TOOLS

ChartSide

Four quick calculators for the numbers you check every shift. Nothing is stored, nothing leaves this page — type in, read out.

01

Body Mass Index

Weight relative to height, used as a quick screening figure — not a diagnosis on its own.

-- kg/m²

--

Formula used

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ [height (m)]²

  • Below 18.5 — Underweight
  • 18.5 – 24.9 — Normal weight
  • 25 – 29.9 — Overweight
  • 30 and above — Obese
02

Drug Dosage (mg/kg)

Works out a total dose from a weight-based order, then splits it into a tablet or vial count.

-- mg total

--

Formula used

Total dose (mg) = weight (kg) × ordered dose (mg/kg)

Units needed = total dose (mg) ÷ strength per unit (mg)

This is a teaching tool for working through the maths. Always check any real dose against a second qualified person and the prescription itself.
